Privacy Policy

Chronology (com.gitgud.chronology) · Effective 6 August 2026

Chronology is a history card game. It is designed to work without knowing who you are. This page explains exactly what data the app handles, why, and how to get it deleted.

What we collect

An anonymous identifier. When you first open the app, an anonymous account is created for your install. It is a random identifier. It is not linked to your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, or any other personal detail, because the app never asks for any of those.

Gameplay statistics. Your scores, games played, cards you have seen, mistakes, category accuracy, and rank progress are stored against that anonymous identifier so your progress and Museum collection survive between sessions.

Online match data. If you play online, room codes and the moves made in a match are processed on our servers to run the game between the two players.

Crash reports. If the app crashes or hits an error, a report is sent to Sentry, an error tracking service. It includes technical details such as the device model, operating system version, app version, and what went wrong in the code. Crash reports are used only to find and fix bugs.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, or phone number
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, or files
  • No advertising identifiers, and no ads at all
  • No tracking across other apps or websites

Where your data lives

Gameplay data is stored on Supabase, a hosted database service. Crash reports are stored on Sentry. All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS and secure WebSockets). We keep routine database backups for up to 60 days for disaster recovery.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with anyone. The only third parties that touch it are Supabase and Sentry, acting as our infrastructure providers as described above. Data is never used for advertising.

Requesting deletion of your data

To have the data Chronology holds about you deleted, email kuroi@playgitgudgames.com with the subject "Delete my Chronology data". Because accounts are anonymous, we cannot look you up by name, so we will work with you to identify the records tied to your install.

On a confirmed request we delete the anonymous account and all gameplay statistics stored against it. Copies in routine database backups expire within 60 days. Crash reports on Sentry are retained for up to 90 days before being discarded automatically; they identify a device, not a person.

Uninstalling the app abandons the anonymous account; the stats attached to it are no longer reachable from any device.

Children

Chronology is an educational card game suitable for all ages. It collects the same minimal, anonymous data from every player regardless of age, and no personal information from anyone.
